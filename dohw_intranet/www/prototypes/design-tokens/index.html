Three directions for restyling DoWH's components in frappe.io's idiom

Same content, same DoWH Gold/dark/Inter identity, three takes on how far to carry frappe.io's restraint — dense 13–15px type, a loose 4px spacing scale, flat/near-shadowless surfaces, color held back as a rare accent.

A — Frappe.io literal

Near-monochrome surfaces, bare text-link actions, chromeless list-style cards, hollow outline badges. Gold appears only as a 2px underline accent.
