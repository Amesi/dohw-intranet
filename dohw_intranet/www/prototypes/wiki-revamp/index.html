Culvert Design Standards

This document sets out the minimum design standards for culvert installations on Highways Wing road projects, effective from the 2026 construction season.

Sizing requirements

Culvert diameter must be sized for a 1-in-50-year flood event, with a minimum freeboard of 300mm above the design water level.

Material specification

Reinforced concrete pipe (RCP) is the default material; corrugated steel pipe (CSP) requires Divisional Engineer sign-off.
